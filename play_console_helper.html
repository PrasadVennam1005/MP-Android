Google Play Console Setup Guide

Follow this step-by-step checklist to configure licensing, in-app purchases, and recurring subscriptions in the Google Play Console for MoneyPilot.

Step 1

Deploy Build to Internal Testing

Google Play requires you to upload an Android App Bundle (AAB) containing the Billing permission to a testing track before you can create billing products.

Build the release bundle: Run ./gradlew bundleRelease in your terminal.
Open Google Play Console and select your app.
Navigate to Testing > Internal testing.
Create a new release and upload your AAB bundle. Publish the internal release.
Step 2

Configure Lifetime Premium Upgrade

Create a one-time in-app product to represent the lifetime premium upgrade.

Product ID
premium_upgrade_lifetime_1499
Product Name
MoneyPilot Premium (Lifetime)
Navigate to Monetize > Products > One-time products.
Click Create product.
Paste the Product ID copied above.
Enter the details, set the price, and click Save and then Activate.
Step 3

Configure Monthly Subscription

Create a subscription product to support monthly recurring billing.

Product ID
premium_subscription_monthly
Product Name
MoneyPilot Premium (Monthly)
Navigate to Monetize > Products > Subscriptions.
Click Create subscription.
Use the subscription Product ID copied above.
Add a Base Plan, set it to Auto-Renew, and define the monthly pricing.
Click Save and then Activate.
Step 4

Configure App License Key

Retrieve your app's public licensing key to authorize secure signature checking.

In your Play Console, go to Monetize > Play Games Services > Setup and management > Configuration (or Monetization setup).
Find the Licensing & in-app billing section and copy the Base64 public key.
